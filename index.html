Widen for iPhone Duo

Close your iPhone Duo and Safari gives up nearly a fifth of the outer display. Widen gets it back.

You paid a lot for your iPhone Duo; you should get all of the display you paid for. With the iPhone Duo closed, Safari stacks its buttons in a narrow column beneath the camera, and every website stops well short of it. That’s a band about 250 pixels wide showing nothing. Widen is a compact Safari extension that lets pictures, tables and page backgrounds run to the edge of the outer display, and, if you choose, text too. It’s like getting a display over 20% wider.

A closer look

Wider, without the side effects

Stretching a page is the easy part. Widen spends most of its effort on what happens next.

  1. Pictures reach the edge. In the standard Pictures mode, photos, maps, tables and backgrounds fill the full width behind Safari’s see-through buttons, while text runs up to them and stops, so nothing you’re reading is hidden.
  2. Text too, if you choose. Prefer every line to run the full width? Choose Pictures & text, for every site or just the ones you pick. It’s your call.
  3. The camera keeps its corner. A site’s own top bar is kept just short of the column, so its menu and search buttons never end up behind the lens.
  4. Scrolling stays straight. A page wider than the window would normally wobble left and right as you scroll; Widen holds it steady. Pinch-zoom is untouched.

Widen works on almost every website. If one doesn’t look right, tell us and we’ll try to fix it.

Portrait or landscape, it’s the same. When you turn the iPhone Duo so Safari’s buttons end up on the left, Widen swaps sides with them. And when you open the iPhone Duo, Widen goes quiet: the inner display doesn’t need it.

Why Widen

More features than any other iPhone Duo Safari extension

Simpler extensions for the iPhone Duo give you just one on/off switch for every site. Widen gives you three choices: Off, Pictures and Pictures & text. Then it adds the things you only miss once you’ve used them.

Nothing hidden behind the buttons
In Pictures mode, text runs up to Safari’s buttons and stops, so you never have to nudge the page to read the end of a line. Pictures & text is there if you want every line full width.
Portrait, landscape or both
Choose whether Widen works in portrait, in landscape, or in both.
A setting for each site
Leave a site on your usual choice, or give it its own: Off, Pictures or Pictures & text. The panel lists every site you’ve set, so you can change or remove any of them.
Fix and widen desktop-only sites
Sites built only for computers are skipped, unless you ask. Turn it on for one, and Widen first shrinks the page to fit, then widens it like any other.
Clear the clutter
A small display and pop-ups don’t mix. An optional setting hides the cookie notices and “join our mailing list” boxes that pin themselves over the page.
Shortcuts
Set Widen picks Off, Pictures or Pictures & text; Toggle Widen flips between Off and Pictures. Use them in your own shortcuts, in automations, or on the Action button.
Siri
Say “Toggle Widen” or “Set Widen”, without opening Safari’s menu.
Control Center
Switch modes without opening Safari’s menu. Add the Widen button to Control Center; each tap steps through Off, Pictures and Pictures & text, and the button shows which is on.
Under 1 MB
About half a megabyte to download. Light enough you’ll forget it’s there.
No subscription
Pay once and it’s yours for life, on every iPhone Duo signed in to your Apple Account. Nothing renews.
Private by design
No account to create and no tracking. Your settings never leave your iPhone.
The settings

Everything in one panel, right in Safari

Choose Widen from Safari’s page menu and every option is there, on the page you’re looking at. No digging through the Settings app.

Start

From download to wider pages

  1. In the Widen app

    Tap Safari Settings.

  2. In Settings

    Switch Allow Extension on and set All Websites to Allow.

  3. In Safari

    Close your iPhone Duo and browse. Every site opens in Pictures mode.
