Widen for iPhone Duo

Close your iPhone Duo and Safari gives up nearly a fifth of the outer display. Widen gets it back.

You paid a lot for your iPhone Duo; you should get all of the display you paid for. With the Duo closed, Safari stacks its buttons in a narrow column by the camera, and every website stops well short of it. That’s a band about 250 pixels wide showing nothing. Widen is a compact Safari extension that lets pictures, tables and page backgrounds run to the edge of the outer display, and text right up to Safari’s buttons.

A closer look

Wider, without the side effects

Stretching a page is the easy part. Widen spends most of its effort on what happens next.

  1. Pictures reach the edge; words stop at the buttons. In the standard Pictures mode, photos, maps, tables and backgrounds fill the full width behind Safari’s see-through buttons, while text runs right up to them and stops, so nothing you’re reading is hidden. Prefer every line full width? Choose Pictures & text instead.
  2. The camera keeps its corner. A site’s own top bar is kept just short of the column, so its menu and search buttons never end up behind the lens.
  3. Scrolling stays straight. A page wider than the window would normally wobble left and right as you scroll; Widen holds it steady. Pinch-zoom is untouched.

Widen works on almost every website. If one doesn’t look right, tell us and we’ll try to fix it.

Portrait or landscape, it’s the same. When you turn the Duo so Safari’s buttons end up on the left, Widen swaps sides with them. And when you open the Duo, Widen goes quiet: the inner display doesn’t need it.

Why Widen

More features than any other iPhone Duo Safari extension

Simpler extensions for the iPhone Duo give you just one on/off switch for every site. Widen gives you three choices: Off, Pictures and Pictures & text. Then it adds the things you only miss once you’ve used them.

Nothing hidden behind the buttons
In Pictures mode, text runs up to Safari’s buttons and stops, so you never have to nudge the page to read the end of a line. Pictures & text is there if you want every line full width.
Portrait, landscape or both
Choose whether Widen works in portrait, in landscape, or in both.
A setting for each site
Leave a site on your usual choice, or give it its own: Off, Pictures or Pictures & text. The panel lists every site you’ve set, so you can change or remove any of them.
Desktop-only sites, if you like
Sites built only for computers are skipped, unless you ask. Turn it on for one, and Widen first shrinks the page to fit, then widens it like any other.
Clear the clutter
A small display and pop-ups don’t mix. An optional setting hides the cookie notices and “join our mailing list” boxes that pin themselves over the page.
Shortcuts
Set Widen picks Off, Pictures or Pictures & text; Toggle Widen flips between Off and Pictures. Use them in your own shortcuts, in automations, or on the Action button.
Siri
Say “Toggle Widen” or “Set Widen”, without opening Safari’s menu.
Under 1 MB
About half a megabyte to download. Light enough you’ll forget it’s there.
Private by design
No account to create, no subscription, and no tracking. Your settings never leave your iPhone.
Start

From download to wider pages

  1. In the Widen app

    Open Widen and tap Open Safari Settings. It takes you straight to the right page in the Settings app, because only you can switch on a Safari extension.

  2. In Settings

    Switch Allow Extension on, then set All Websites to Allow. Back in the Widen app, start your free week; a tick shows against each step once it’s done.

  3. In Safari

    Close your Duo and browse. Every site opens in Pictures mode. To change that, or give one site its own setting, choose Widen from the menu in Safari’s address bar.
