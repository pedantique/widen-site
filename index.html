Widen for iPhone Duo

Close your Duo and Safari gives up nearly a fifth of the screen. Widen gets it back.

With the Duo closed, Safari stacks its buttons in a narrow column by the camera, and every website stops well short of it. That’s 84 points of good glass showing nothing. Widen is a small Safari extension that lets pictures, tables and page backgrounds run to the edge of the display, and text right up to Safari’s buttons.

A closer look

Wider, without the side effects

Stretching a page is the easy part. Widen spends most of its effort on what happens next.

  1. Pictures reach the edge; words stop at the buttons. Photos, maps, tables and backgrounds fill the full width behind Safari’s see-through buttons. Text runs right up to them but never underneath, so nothing you’re reading is hidden. (Prefer every line full width? One switch in the panel allows it.)
  2. The camera keeps its corner. A site’s own top bar is kept just short of the column, so its menu and search buttons never end up behind the lens.
  3. Scrolling stays straight. A page wider than the window would normally wobble left and right as you scroll; Widen holds it steady. Pinch-zoom is untouched.

It also follows the phone round in portrait and both landscape orientations, including the one where Safari moves its buttons to the left. And when you open the Duo, Widen goes quiet: the big screen doesn’t need it.

Why Widen

More than a single switch

Simpler extensions for the Duo give you one on/off switch for every site. Widen starts there, then adds the things you only miss once you’ve used them.

Nothing hidden behind the buttons
Text runs up to Safari’s buttons and stops, so you never have to nudge the page to read the end of a line.
Always or Never, site by site
Leave Widen on and mark the odd site Never. Or leave it off and mark only your daily reads Always. Each site’s setting sticks until you change it.
Desktop-only sites, if you like
Sites built only for computers are skipped unless you ask. Turn it on for one, and Widen first shrinks the page to fit, then widens it like any other.
Portrait, landscape or both
Two switches choose whether Widen works in portrait, in landscape, or in both.
Clear the clutter
Small screens and pop-ups don’t mix. An optional setting hides the cookie notices and “join our mailing list” boxes that pin themselves over the page.
Shortcuts and Siri
Two Shortcuts actions, Set Widen and Toggle Widen, so an automation or the Action button can switch it for you.
Yours, privately
No account, no subscription, no analytics. Your settings stay on your iPhone.
Start

From download to wider pages

  1. Open the app

    Tap Open Safari Settings. iOS only lets you, not apps, switch on a Safari extension, so this takes you to the right switch.

  2. Allow it

    Turn on Allow Extension and set All Websites to Allow.

  3. Turn on Widen

    With the Duo closed, tap the menu button in Safari’s address bar, choose Widen, and tap the wide picture.
