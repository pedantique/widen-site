Privacy Policy

Widen for iPhone Duo, by Challis & Company Pty Limited · Last updated 21 October 2026

Widen is designed so that your data stays with you. We don’t collect, store or sell any personal information.

In short: Widen collects nothing about you and doesn’t send your data anywhere. Your settings stay on your iPhone, and Apple handles the purchase.

What Widen does on a web page

Widen is a Safari extension. To change how wide a page is laid out, Safari lets it run a small script on the pages you visit, if you allow it. That script measures the page and the display and adjusts the layout on your iPhone. It does not read, record or send what is on the page, what you type or where you browse.

What is stored, and where

  • Your settings: your choice of Off, Pictures or Pictures & text, any settings for particular sites, which orientations it works in, and whether banner hiding is on. These are kept on your iPhone, shared only between the Widen app and its Safari extension so that Shortcuts can change them. A site’s name is saved only when you make a rule for it.
  • Your free week: the date it started is kept on your iPhone, in its keychain, so reinstalling doesn’t restart it. It’s never sent anywhere, and erasing the iPhone clears it.
  • Your purchase: buying Widen is an App Store transaction, recorded by Apple against your Apple Account. Widen keeps a copy of its status on your iPhone so the Safari extension knows whether it may run.

None of this is sent to us. The Widen app and extension don’t talk to any server of ours.

Network activity

The only connection Widen makes is to Apple’s App Store, to check your purchase. It doesn’t contact us, doesn’t load remote code, and doesn’t report the pages you visit.

Analytics and advertising

None. Widen contains no analytics, tracking or advertising code of its own, and doesn’t identify you or your device.

If you’ve chosen in your iPhone’s settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage figures, such as how many people installed Widen. You control that in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Purchases

The one-time purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, name or Apple Account. Apple’s own privacy policy covers it.

If you email us

If you contact support, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to reply. We don’t add you to any list.

This website

This site has no cookies, analytics or tracking. It’s hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may briefly log visitors’ IP addresses for security. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Children

Widen collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated first and the date at the top revised.

Contact

Questions about privacy: see the support page.
