Widen for iPhone Duo

Devices and browsers

Which phone models does it work on?

The iPhone Duo, on its outer display when the phone is closed. That’s the only screen where Safari puts its buttons in a column down the side. When you open the Duo, Widen leaves pages alone, and on other iPhone models it has nothing to do.

Which browsers does it work in?

Safari. Widen is a Safari extension, and other browsers on iPhone can’t run Safari extensions.

Using it

Isn’t this what Request Desktop Website does?

No. A desktop page is shrunk to fit, so the text gets tiny. Widen keeps the phone layout at its normal size and simply gives it more room.

Does any text end up behind Safari’s buttons?

No. Text runs up to the buttons and stops; only pictures, tables and backgrounds go behind them. If you’d rather have every line full width, turn off Keep text clear of Safari’s buttons in Widen’s panel.

Will it break websites?

It leaves alone any page that fixes its own width or comes only in a desktop layout. If a site still looks odd, set it to Never in Widen’s panel and tell us which one.

Can I keep one site the way it was?

Yes. Open Widen’s panel on that site and choose Never. Or do the reverse: leave Widen off and set the few sites you read most to Always.

Can a shortcut turn it on and off?

Yes. Look for Set Widen and Toggle Widen in the Shortcuts app. Open pages follow the new setting straight away.

How do I turn it off?

Choose Off in Widen’s panel in Safari. To remove it altogether, turn off Allow Extension in Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions › Widen, or delete the app.

Privacy

Why does Widen need access to all websites?

It has to adjust each page’s layout as it loads. It doesn’t keep or send anything: no analytics, and no network requests of its own. The privacy policy is short.

Price

When does the free week start?

The first time you turn on Widen in Safari, not when you download the app.

What happens when the free week ends?

Pages go back to how Safari lays them out, and your settings wait for you. Buy Widen in the app whenever you like and everything picks up where it was.

Is it a subscription?

No. At our launch price it’s US$3.99 for lifetime access, paid once. Outside the US, the App Store sets the price in your currency.

I have a new iPhone. Do I pay again?

No. Open the Widen app on the new phone, signed in with the same Apple Account, and restore your purchase.
