FAQ

Widen for iPhone Duo

Devices and browsers

Which phone models does it work on?

The iPhone Duo, on its outer display, when the phone is closed. That’s the only display where Safari puts its buttons in a column down the side. On the inner display, Widen leaves pages alone, and on other iPhone models the app tells you it has nothing to change.

Which browsers does it work in?

Safari. Widen is a Safari extension, and other browsers on iPhone can’t run Safari extensions.

Using it

Isn’t this what Request Desktop Website does?

No. Request Desktop Website swaps a phone layout for the computer version and shrinks it to fit, so the text gets tiny. Widen keeps the phone layout at its normal size and gives it more room.

Does Widen help sites that only have a desktop layout?

Yes, if you ask. Those sites are left alone at first. Set one to Pictures or Pictures & text and tick Widen its desktop layout too: Widen fits the page to the display, then stretches it across the full width like any other.

Does any text end up behind Safari’s buttons?

Not in the standard Pictures mode: text runs up to the buttons and stops, and only pictures, tables and backgrounds go behind them. If you’d rather have every line full width, choose Pictures & text. Site headers and menus stay clear of the camera either way.

What are Off, Pictures and Pictures & text?

Off leaves Safari as usual. Pictures lets photos, backgrounds and tables use the full width, with text stopping at Safari’s buttons. Pictures & text lets text run under the buttons too. You can choose one for every site, and a different one for particular sites.

Will it work on the sites I read?

On almost all of them. A few sites size themselves in ways Widen can’t reach yet, or come only in a desktop layout, which Widen leaves alone unless you ask. If a site doesn’t look right, set it to Off in Widen’s panel and tell us which one; we’ll try to fix it.

Can I keep one site the way it was?

Yes. Open Widen’s panel on that site and set it to Off. Or do the reverse: leave Widen off and set the few sites you read most to Pictures or Pictures & text. The panel lists every site you’ve set, with a Remove button.

Can a shortcut switch it?

Yes. In the Shortcuts app, Set Widen chooses Off, Pictures or Pictures & text, and Toggle Widen switches between Off and Pictures. Use them in your own shortcuts, in automations or on the Action button. Open pages follow straight away.

Can I use Siri?

Yes. Say “Toggle Widen” to switch between Off and Pictures, or “Set Widen” to pick a mode.

How do I get Safari’s normal layout back?

Choose Off in Widen’s panel in Safari. To remove it altogether, turn off Allow Extension in Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions › Widen, or delete the app.

Safari settings

Where are Widen’s settings in Safari?

In Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions › Widen. The Widen app’s Open Safari Settings button takes you straight there.

What does each switch do?

Allow Extension is the main switch: with it off, Widen does nothing at all. Under it, All Websites should be set to Allow so Widen can work on every page. Ask makes Safari check with you site by site, and until you allow a site, Widen can’t see it (its panel tells you how to allow it). Private Browsing is a separate switch, covered below.

Does it work in Private Browsing?

Only if you let it. Safari doesn’t run extensions in Private Browsing unless you switch on Private Browsing in Widen’s Safari settings. Either way, Widen keeps nothing about the pages you visit.

Safari says Widen can “read and alter web pages and see your browsing history”. Should I worry?

That’s Safari’s standard wording for any extension that works on web pages. Widen only measures each page’s layout and adjusts it on your iPhone. It doesn’t record your browsing or send anything anywhere. The privacy policy has the details.

Do I have to choose anything in Safari first?

No. Once Widen is allowed and your free week has started, every site opens in Pictures. Use the Widen panel in Safari’s page menu only to change that, or to give one site its own setting.

Privacy

Why does Widen need access to all websites?

It has to adjust each page’s layout as it loads. It doesn’t keep or send anything: no analytics, and no network requests of its own. The privacy policy is short.

Price

When does the free week start?

When you start it in the Widen app. It’s tied to your Apple Account, so deleting and reinstalling the app doesn’t give you another one.

My free week is over. Now what?

Pages go back to how Safari lays them out, and your settings wait for you. Buy Widen in the app whenever you like and everything picks up where it was.

Is it a subscription?

No. At our launch price it’s US$3.99 for lifetime access, paid once. Outside the US, the App Store sets the equivalent price in your currency.

I have a second iPhone Duo. Do I pay again?

No. Open the Widen app on the other Duo, signed in with the same Apple Account, and restore your purchase.
